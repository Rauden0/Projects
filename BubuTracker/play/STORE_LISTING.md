## Full description
BubuTracker helps friends and family stay in sync with optional, consent-based location sharing.

• Sign in securely with Auth0  
• See people you track on a map  
• Approve or reject tracking requests  
• Revoke access anytime  

Location is only shared with users you accept. You control who can see you.
