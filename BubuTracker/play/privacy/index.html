BubuTracker Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 September 2026 · Android app ID: com.martinmucka.bubutracker · iOS app ID: com.martinmucka.bubutrackerios

This privacy policy describes how BubuTracker (“the App”) handles information when you use the Android or iOS application and its backend API.

1. Who we are

BubuTracker is a location-sharing app that lets signed-in users optionally share their device location with people they approve.

2. Information we collect

CategoryExamplesWhy
AccountEmail, Auth0 user id, optional nameCreate and identify your account
AuthenticationAccess/refresh tokens (encrypted on device)Keep you signed in
LocationGPS while sharing is activeShow your position to people you allowed
Tracking relationshipsEmails and accept/reject/revoke decisionsManage who can see whom

We do not sell personal data. We do not use your location for advertising.

3. How location sharing works

  • Location is uploaded only while you are signed in and the App is open.
  • Others see your location only after you accept their tracking request.
  • You can revoke access in the App at any time.

4. Third parties

  • Auth0 — sign-in
  • OpenStreetMap — map tile imagery (anonymous tile requests only)
  • Google Play services (Fused Location) — device location on Android
  • Google Play / Apple App Store — distribution and optional vitals

5. Your choices

Deny location permission, reject/revoke tracking, sign out, or permanently delete your account and all its data any time from the App’s profile screen (or by contacting the email below).
